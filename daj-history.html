DAJ History

137 - 145 ABY Era Of the Split


Major Events:

Sith Order is destroyed by Cade Skywalker and co. Cade dies in the end leaving no one to unite the Jedi, split in the conflict.

Dac Rebuilds

Jedi Order splits, Everyone has different views and wants to go their own way.

DAJ is created with the destruction of Kaliban and is established as the Largest Order in the Galaxy centralized on Coruscant.

Galactic Alliance becomes the Majority in the Senate with Chief of State Drexdo Braklen, Muun, Age 45

Imperial Knights become Tightly knit with the GA

Commonwealth (CW) peacefully Separates to control itself independently

Mandalorians now have their own Sector, Peace treaties are worked out with the GA and CW

Hutts earn the same rights as Mandalorians.

Details/Fine Print:

Sith
After the Destruction of the Sith Order and acknowledgment of the error of their ways they split into many different groups. The different Groups Reverted to the Rule of Two that was established by Bane all those years ago. Now for each different group of Sith specialize in different areas for any individual who is universally talented wont be as talented as one who specializes in one area.
The Sith pairs are as follows:

Derriphan-Jen: Dark Devourers, This Pair that are much more inclined in saber skills. They can put any duelist to shame with their viscous strikes. They focus more on using the Force to increase their power and strength allowing them to fight for almost days on end without getting weary.
Current Location: Prakith, Temple of the Immortal God of the Sith, AKA Andeddu Temple.

Taral-Jen: Protectors of Darkness, This pair works more with attacking from the shadows. They can conceal their presence in the Force and remain hidden from others. They strike from the darkness and can even turn invisible when it suits their needs.
Current Location: Dathomir, Self-constructed Hideout in the forest, Protected by the Force.

Saarai-Jen: Dark Truth, This pair focuses more on the ancient Sith Sorcery and raw force power. They can Prolong life, Manipulate it with Sith Alchemy, create devastating images of your worst fears before your eyes and drive you insane within a second, and the most powerful can summon tentacles of darkness that can slice through the strongest of substances like they were butter.
Current Location: Ambria, Banes Old Camp, Protection: Force spells.

The 6 Sith refuse to leave hiding and cling to their powers and beliefs. The Masters of each meet once a Decade to create plans for the future and to ensure their treaties and peaces still remain intact.

Dac
Dac gets rebuilt and is awarded a hefty amount of credits for the rebuilding from the GA because of the damages done by the sith. The cleanup of bodies was also performed by the GA. Full compensation for all damages is being covered by the Dac Gov. and GA. An Order of Jedi more focused on using their powers for reconstructing and helping others to move to Dac. They take an active role in the reconstruction of the broken cities. Later on, DAJ will build a temple on Dac.

The Jedi Orders
With Cade Skywalker dead there was no willing Skywalker (see Nat Skywalker for details) to unite The Jedi Order split into 5 main Groups. All ranging in leniency with the Code and beliefs. The middle of them are the ones who remain with the Code and Laws of the original order. There were two prominent Orders in the Galaxy at the time, DAJ and JoF.

DAJ
The Dark Angels are known as the "Unforgiven", though few within the Galaxy know why. The legends say they hunt for their wayward brethren, known as the "Fallen". The Dark Angels are determined to bring all the Fallen to justice, often to the exclusion of any other mission.

How this situation came to pass is a story that the Dark Angels tell no one. They are highly suspicious of outsiders and are often perceived as aloof by their Rebel comrades. Sufficed to say, the shame of the Dark Angels tale drives them to erase it from the memories of all. If they can track down every Fallen Dark Angel, the Dark Angels hope that they can redeem themselves in the eyes of the Force.

In their efforts to hunt the Fallen while defending the Galaxy, the Dark Angels have several unique companies. The 1st Company, known as the Death wing, is made of veterans with a penchant for stubborn resistance. The 2nd Company, known as Raven wing, is composed of Fast Attack and they specialize in scouting and shadow work. These two unique units give the Dark Angels an edge on the battlefield. Additionally, Wolf Wing and Crane Wings were also formed later on, the former for fast skirmishes and the latter for Jedi with diplomatic skills.

Ultimately, however, the power of the Dark Angels lies in their stubborn refusal to retreat in situations where even other Jedi would withdraw. Their dogged resistance against overwhelming odds is legendary.

The ancient home world of the Dark Angels, Caliban, was destroyed during the great heresy, when a Sith attempted to influence the very nature of the force to the dark side. The primarch of the Dark Angels, Azrael was supposedly killed in the attack on Caliban. Luckily his two apprentices at the time escaped, Mutius Vos and Kavar Orion. They quickly returned to the fighting and helped put an end to the war. When it did, they both re-created DAJ in memory of Azrael. They quickly gathered many people who wanted to join and had the largest Order in the Galaxy in the blink of an eye.

The Galactic Alliance
The Galactic Alliance worked on getting representatives to all known planets still part of the Alliance. The Alliance also worked on modernizing all planets that were behind in technology, hospitals and defensive technology. The GA was still stirred up about the damage done by the previous Sith Order, they amped up their defenses and improved all battle ships, trooper armor etc. and were alert for quite a while. They made sure to be friends with the Different divisions of Jedi Orders and Imperial Knights.

The Order of Imperial Knights
The Imperial Knights (Roan Fell Included) decided to become better friend with the new GA. Roan Fell was the Chief of states advisor and right hand man for anything and everything as he previously was the Chief of State. Most other Senior Knights were Diplomats, Senator Guardians or on occasion Senate representatives for their home planet.

The Mandalorians
The Mandalorians were granted their own sector in space and given their own Government. The GA helped establish it them pulled out from the sector entirely. The Mandalorians now stand as allies to the GA and Commonwealth. Mandalorian space exact boundaries are being worked out.

The Hutts
Hutts were given their own sector in space. The Kessel mines and Ylesia were awarded to The Hutts for they are rich in spice mines and the Spice business was awarded to them. This is because the Hutts were helping the Jedi get rid of the Sith. The sith were hampering their business and much more strict than the Previous GA was. The Hutts now are independent from the GA and they were completely withdrawn from the sector.

Major Events:

Tensions Rise between Hutt and Mandalorian Government

Killik Planets Die. Investigation takes place by GA

3 Members of the Council of First Knowledge, 1 Weapon Master and 2 Shadows (6 Jedi Masters Total) disappear in the Deep Core From Ashla Jedi Order (top of the list for strictness)

DAJ wages two wars: with BD and CE clans


Details/Fine Print:

The Galactic Alliance and the Chiss
For reasons unknown to the Galactic Alliance and Commonwealth, the Chiss Ascendancy expanded their borders outwards. After continuous attempts at communication that failed (the messages were not getting through). Chief of State decided to go on a diplomatic mission with the company of Roan Fel and 2 other Imperial Knights. Upon discussion of whats happening the Chiss explained that they found EMPs, and disruptive equipment that could take out a Capital Ship surrounding the Chiss border. Further analysis had shown they had GA and CW symbols imprinted on shortly before being planted, it was also found that the make of the devices were neither GA nor CW. The GA is now helping the Chiss Ascendancy clean up the mess and are putting together investigation units to find out what happened and how.

Hutts and Mandalorians
The Hutts and Mandalorians have recently entered a dispute over topics most would call trivial. The Mandalorians were upset that Hutts were promoting Mandalorian Bounty Hunters for their own personal use. Mandalorians were against this because it was tearing the clans apart. A tournament was held to have a new Mandalore brought into power to have full power over the clans, even to bring them all into war.

Killiks and their Sad End
Upon inspection of the Killik planets an unforeseeable tragedy occurred to the nanotech bio cells that had been previously planted there by the GA. They had initially been planted to keep the Killiks from their natual habit of expanding incredibly in little time. The Nanotech cells had broken down and the natural plant life had become so reliant on it that when the cells broke down and died so did all naturally occuring life on the planet.

The Ossus Project
The Ossus Project was originally started by Kol Skywalker (Cades father), the intent was to rebuild the environmental structure of a battle ravaged planet, or to change the state of an uninhabitable one such as Tatooine. The project worked with the Vong Forming technology, unfortunately its first test was sabotaged by Darth Maladi (Sith version of a Jedi Healer, for more info on her search the archives, or search Cilghal, a famous Jedi healer). With the project under the watchful eyes of Both Imperial Knights and GA it is being worked with to not only recreate plant life on a planet but create life itself. Planets being tested on are currently: Tatooine, Dantooine and Rhen Var.

6 dead Jedi not 1 reason
A ship was found with a sec destination to Coruscant. When the ship landed at the Jedi temple 6 dead Jedi Masters were found in their seats. Upon investigation by the Jedi they have no results from the Autopsy, There is no scorch marks, blood stains, signs of struggle, or anything out of the normal of any kind. The strangest part of them all is the only thing left to suspect to have killed them is a Dark Force user, the only thing that outrules that is the fact that theres no trace of Dark Force in the ship or on the bodies. Further research is being done into the matter.

DAJ - BD war

Some promising members have decided to leave the order, thinking that their skills are being restricted. This resulted in a conflict know to cost many lives and good reputation of the DAJ order. In the following years this situation will happen several times giving DAJ a reputation of a war-hungry order with ineffective leadership.

DAJ - CE war

Zell Almasy was a gifted student whose abilities rivaled even the Masters. One day he was called to the Council chambers and informed that he was promoted to Master, however, the legend says, he slew the Councilor that told him this (Daedlus Nohasi) and fled from the temple forming CE (Crimson empire). Regardless if the legend is true or not, DAJ waged wars with CE. Some blame this on Kavars lust for war. Hiko (Sasuke) was the leader of wolf wing at the time: the wing with most skirmishes.


160 - 180 ABY Era Of the Scars

Major Events:

The EMPs and disruptive equipment found on Chiss Ascendancy borders are investigated.

Hutts and Mandalorians resolve their conflicts.

Reports from Zonama Sekot that Killiks were found on the planet.

Black Sun attacks Hutt spice mines.


Several important events in DAJ occur, including the first child in the order and the first GrandMaster elections.

Details/Fine Print:

EMPs and Disruptive equipment
GA inspection shows that part of the hull of the devices were built from 38.7229% percent Mandalorian Iron. This is however not the standard for EMP devices manufactured in Mandalorian space, by Mandalorians or for any other product made to withstand the vacuum of space made by Mandalorians. It was also found that the Mandalorian insignia on the inside of the devices was placed at an angle not normally found on Mandalorian devices such as these. Oddly enough it was found that the disruptors were pointed away from Chiss space so that if anyone were to near Chiss space their ships would be disabled and unable to do anything.

Hutts and Mandalorians
The Hutts and Mandalorians Resolved their conflict with what some would find as a rather odd deal. Both parties agreed that should anyone living in Hutt Space break Mandalorian Law the Mandalorians get to handle it and Vice Versa. They also agreed that should the Hutts place a bounty on someones head and said person appear in Mandalorian space the Mandalorians are to hand the target over. In short the 2 groups have decided to be fair, even though the original reason the Mandalorians were upset was that their skills were being exploited into Mecenaries. The Hutts decided to lighten up and enlisted in other groups and races known for their fighting prowess.

Killiks you say?
Zonoma Sekot contacted the Ashla Council and notified them that a group of Killiks was spotted. A recon group was sent to investigate, but they did not manage to confirm it.

Black Sun attacks on Hutt spice mines.

Black Sun pirates began raiding Hutt spice mining facilities after realizing the Hutts were in no shape to respond. Hutts have requested the help from both GA and the CW. GA hesitates to help, hoping to bargain for more profitable contracts. Strangely, CW responds to the request very fast and sends small security forces. This makes CW and the Hutts to trust each other much more, while GA is now losing its reputation.

DAJ events

-DAJ conflicts with Kain Yukishiro and his clan.
-Mutius marries Serenity. First child is born within DAJ academy walls: Cortana Vos. Jedi Council Members of the time: Mutius, Zaraz, Kavar, Blue, Kevin, Stephens.
-Kavars own inner conflict results in his leave and attempt to destroy DAJ. Later he manages to return to light and comes back.
-First GrandMaster elections. Kavar is elected. Kavar gets way too much power and slowly gets corrupted.

180 - 200 ABY Era of the Uncertainty

Major Events:

Several Jedi spies are caught spying for the strange Dark and Grey Jedi cults.

The great schism: Formation of the Jensaarai Jedi Alliance. JJA-DAJ Conflict.

DAJ allies with JoF

Hutts refuse to grand GA clearance to pass through their space

Mandalorians have issues with CW regarding the trade routes and equipment sale sanctions

Details/Fine Print:

Unconfirmed spies

Several Jedi spies are caught spying for the strange Dark and Grey Jedi cults which suddenly become widespread throughout the Galaxy. Jedi leaders deny their involvement and claim that the spies went there without authorization. The ignorance towards the cults only helps their cause and attracts more followers. While it is unknown who is behind the cults, Jedi pay little attention to them.

Jensaarai

Ruyk Ruyzaki, a DAJ Knight leaves with a bunch of others to form JJA. The Jensaarai are an order of Force-users originating from the Suarbi system, who arose during the Clone Wars. Their name was Sith for "hidden followers of truth." Their philosophy and methodology was a blend of Sith and Jedi teachings. Their hierarchy loosely resembles that of the Jedi Order. During JJA recreation and early years the Jensaarai were opposed to the Jedi, due to ignorance of both Ruyk and Kavar. DAJ-JJA conflict starts. JJA hatred is directed to Kavar mostly, as the dictator and corrupted leader as they view him. In time the conflict was resolved and the two orders parted their ways.

The Strong Alliance

After conflicts regarding strange spies and Jensaarai, DAJ and JoF form a strong union meant to protect both clans and exchange intelligence. Axon and Pra Viilon become friends of Shree Koon and form a trust on personal level between the Orders.

Hutts still remember

When GA deciced to expand their trade routes Hutts refused to grand GA clearance to pass through their space. Their reasons were the absent of any support from GA during the prior years. However, after several terrorist attacks were prevented by DAJ Knights, Hutts attitude towards GA started to improve. Rumors were that the terrorists were hired by GA itself to improve the relations with the Hutts.

Meaningless Sanctions

After the last GA inspection related to EMPs, CW decided to take actions against Mandalorians and try to alter their trading freedoms in CW space. This made little sense, considering that those equipments were found and inspected by GA and had nothing to do with the Mandalorians. Many thought this act was requested by the Hutts who wanted higher shares in the trade business.

200 - 210 ABY The Dark Times

Major events:

Several Splits in DAJ.

GA and CW have confrontations and mistrusts which result in economic crisis.

A group of Mandalorians, later known as Clan Zero, leaves the clans to develop in their own way.

DAJ Collapses. The temple on Coruscant is destroyed.

The Ossus Project stops due to the lack of funding.

Details/Fine Print:

DAJ Splits

Kavar leaves DAJ with others and forms GJO, a split in DAJ. After a while they return and get reaccepted.

Pra Viilon, Vrook, Sedukon and Ptolemy leave and form KoV: a new order, so they could make things the way they want. DAJ Council is indifferent. People lose faith in DAJ. Subsequently, most KoV members return, Pra Viilon disappears in Unknown regions.

The Crisis

It all started when CW made additional sanctions against Mandalorians and then expanded them on GA. Then the Hutts refused to support CW in this move, asking GA for the support. GA however, refused to make any new trade contracts with both the Hutts and Mandalorians. Subsequently, CW stoped all money transactions with GA. This all resulted in a big crisis, which ended when the 4 sides reached an agreement: CW and the Hutts cancelled all sanctions and GA made additional funding of the Mandalorian and CW economics.

Mandalorian Plot

Several Mandalorian groups did not like how the whole galaxy was treating Mandalorians and decided to follow more ancient ways of Mandalorian life. They left and formed so called "Survival Clan" and focused on combat for survival on some harsh worlds. Vron, the leader, refuses to make any contacts with the outer world. Later all contacts with this group are lost.

DAJ Collapse

Kavar returns to DAJ in disguise and tries to form a split in DAJ, leaving with a few members. Serenity returns to DAJ, Mutius avoids her. The rest of the Council are occupied with rumors of spies that have infiltrated DAJ. Subsequently, the Council declares that DAJ no longer functions. Then, all of a sudden, DAJ temple on Coruscant gets destroyed by an earthquake. Origins of the earthquake are still unknown.

No money left

The Ossus project, which did not prove to be as effective as anticipated had been cancelled. The reasons were too big costs and low effectiveness. Plus the Vong technology was not understood well enough. Also the planets chosen for the project were relatively low populated and did not have significant effect on the whole galaxy. Still many planets, such as Dantooine and Tatooine were recovered much faster thanks to the project. Rhen Var remains a cold desert. No life sightings have been spotted after the Ossus project team departed.

210 - 220 ABY Era of the Revival

Major Events:

DAJ is rebuilt

Increased activity in Chiss space

Clan Zero Mandalorians are spotted

Deserted cloning facility found in the Outer Rim

Trade Federation is formed from few worlds of CW and GA

Details/Fine Print:

Dark Angels return

With the help of GA, Axon starts rebuilding DAJ Order. People return from their journeys and build new temple. Pra Viilon returns from the unknown space. DAJ Constitution is accepted. DAJ finally finds a certain path to follow and develop. Second grandmaster elections are held and Mutius is chosen. DAJ expands and builds many temples on many worlds.

Chiss are involved

After several GA investigations in the Chiss space, research and recon teams started disappearing in the region. Chiss Ascendancy could not offer any explanation, while the number of EMPs surrounding the Chiss borders still remains high, and some suspect it is constantly produced somewhere.

Clan Vron?

During some of their travels Imperial Knights have encountered several small Mandalorian factions with old Clan Vron insignia. While Mandalorians were not friendly, Imperial Knights were able to be subtle and not draw much attention. Then they forwarded this information to GA and a joint recon group was sent to investigate. It revealed several Mandalorian clans living in distant worlds and communicating through encrypted channels. Their true motives remain unknown, however they definitely do not like the current Mandalorian status in the galaxy and their politics and they want their own way.

Clones again

A small Jedi recon group was returning from a mission when they found a strange deserted moon with a small energy signature. After a small investigation a facility was found with a lot of medical equipment and genetic material. Kaminoan specialists suggested this was a cloning facility, however what was cloned there, in what quantities, where did all the personnel go and many more questions remain unknown.

220 - 230 ABY Era of the Action (1)

Major Events:

Kaminoans ask GA for better protection

Some Chiss generals try to declare martial law

The red beast virus threatens outer rim worlds

DAJ conflicts with Trade Federation

Vong are sighted in the Outer Rim

Details/Fine Print:

Best Cloners are in trouble

Kaminoans have been unemployed for decades when no orders for clone armies were placed. While they remained friendly with GA, hoping to receive profitable contracts, the pirates and raiders threatened the remote planet of Kamino. Cloning was banned in GA, however the possibility of ordering a clone army for better protection was always being considered. GA decided to send a security force to Kamino to protect them and to ensure they remain loyal to GA.

Chiss Martial Law

Few Chiss general unhappy with the EMPs and lack of action from GA tried to declare martial law to keep their space in order, however the attempt failed when joint Chiss-GA security force stopped the mutiny. Tentions still remain between GA and the Chiss, while EMPs are constantly being found.

Red Beast Virus

When few DAJ Knights encountered an ancient alien ship of unknown design they also found out that the ship crew was infected with an ancient virus known to turn people into mindless red colored creatures. Suddenly infections of this virus were reported on many Outer Rim worlds and GA asked DAJ to help resolve the situation. The investigation showed that some of the pirate and mercenary groups have been releasing the virus and even trying to mutate it into more deadly form. Eventually the epidemic was stopped, however who orchestrated this whole event remains unknown. Red beats virus has mutated into a more deadly form, but the disease is now rarely seen due to proper vaccination ad precautions.

Trade Federation expands

Commonwealth was fully absorbed by the TF and TF became much more powerful force compared to old CW. However in its first days, due to corruption in its the leadership a conflict between TF and DAJ arose after TF security force tried to assassinate DAJ Master Vrook. After several cold war styled events, the corrupted leader of TF was withdrawn from power and DAJ decided to remove all spies from TF. The conflict was over and TF started a more balanced development.

Vong return

Several Vong ships were spotted in distant Outer Rim worlds near the edge of the Unknown regions. While it is known that main Vong groups do not want war anymore and prefer isolation, this group seemed to have rebelled against the peaceful ways and prepares for the offensive.

230 - 240 ABY Era of the Action (2)

Major Events:

OoD and many other terrorist groups are countered by the Jedi

Spies in DAJ

Dac and Kuat shipyards produce new modern battle cruisers for GA

A rebel Vong group attacks GA in a small but powerful offensive

Details/Fine Print:

Terrorism

Terrorists from old TF and corrupted Dark Jedi became much more common in the Galaxy and GA asked the Jedi to help restore the peace. Order of Darkness was one of the most difficult conflicts DAJ faced. While terrorists still are encountered on common basis, the galaxy are relatively safer due to actions DAJ undertook.

Events in DAJ

Mutius and Axon retire from DAJ. Third grandmaster elections are held: Vrook is elected. Some corrupt spies try to create split in DAJ and turn members against The Council. DAJ Council at that time: Vrook, Pra, Mazik, Obi-Won, msc, Rem, Kell. In time the conflict ends with more strict rules being employed inside DAJ Order to counter possible future sabotage.

Vong Offensive

Vong rebel groups have launched massive offensive on GA worlds. Some TF and Hutt planets were also attacked. Main Mandalorian Forces responded at once and managed to secure few Outer Rim worlds to allow GA to regroup and attack. Hutts and TF united managed to clear the Mid Rim, while Jedi and GA launched huge counter attack and retook all major GA worlds. In a last desperate move Vong attacked Coruscant both in space and on ground. Jedi were able to successfully repel the enemy. The Vong generals were captured and the remaining forces surrendered. This conflict showed that despite all the disagreements, main Galactic factions can unite to successfully fight a dangerous enemy.

240 - 245 ABY The Strange Times

Major Events:

Chiss space is isolated and no longer contacts with GA or TF

DAJ numbers run low

Strange attack on GA Hoth base

Unusually big fleets of unknown origins gather in the Outer Rim

Kashyyyk divided: prelude to a big war

GA lifts the ban on cloning, Kaminoans start building an army for GA

Details/Fine Print:

The Chiss isolation

Chiss decided to be isolated from the rest of galactic community. They became weary of countless inspections from GA and stopped all trades with TF. While this move seems of little importance to GA economy, TF has been damaged by this act as their trade with Chiss was the major source of their income. This resulted in loss of trust between TF and GA.

DAJ Events

-Seijitsu and Tarris marry and have a child who becomes second ever born in DAJ: Anallee.

-The travel times: many leave the temple for explorations of uncharted space for search of hidden threat, few return. The remaining DAJ decide to recruit more to increase Jedi numbers.

Attack on Hoth base

GA and DAJ have reconstructed the Hoth base to secure the galaxy sector around the planet. During one of the routine inspections the base was attacked by a strange force. Soldiers with heavy weapons and light walkers launched a raid which was successfully repelled by the Jedi. Master Maziks analysis suggested that the soldiers were clones, who were deployed by their masters into a test battle to check their effectiveness.

Battle of Kashyyyk

Kashyyyk divided! The mysterious new wookiee tribe has abandoned hundred year old wookiee traditions and tries to enforce its ways all over kashyyyk in a violent way. It lives in hidden forest villages, high on the trees, and raids big wookiee cities causing casualties. After peace negotiations failed, Jedi prepared a defense plan for the major wookiee cities, including the capital. During the bloody battle few dark Jedi were spotted, and the commander of rebel wookiees and enemy soldiers was captured. He revealed useful information on the organization of the Sith forces, which are behind all this. However who is directing all this remains unknown. Several sith holocrons were found on kashyyyk and they may be the key to reveal the real enemy.

Clones versus Clones

Jedi were sent to Kamino to find out if the attackers of Hoth base originated from Kamino, but it turned out to be a false statement. Apparently the situation got more complicated since someone else was building clone army. However, Kamino itself was constantly threatened by unknown mercenary groups wishing to steal cloning technologies or to force kaminoans to create a clone army for themselves. In this situation GA ordered to protect Kamino and sent a fleet. Moreover, GA lifted the ban on cloning and ordered a clone army to be built on Kamino to counter the clones being built against GA.
