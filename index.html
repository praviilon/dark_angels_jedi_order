About DAJ

Dark Angels Jedi Order is Jedi Academy Role Play Clan which has been around for more than 5 years. We RP the life of Jedi, training, missions and fun. The RP rules, ranks and other useful information about DAJ can be found in DAJ Constitution. New recruits are accepted as Padawans. They have to attend classes and train under guidance of a Master or Knight. When their Master thinks they are ready, they are given The Trials: The Trial of Courage, The Trial of Skill, The Trial of Spirit. If they successfully pass, they become Knights - the backbone of our order. Our clan has many Jedi professions: from healers and historians to tacticians and pilots.

DAJ is RP clan with some hangout and community elements. Community elements means people can register non-Jedi character at DAJ forum and RP with DAJ members. Hangout elements means there can be some fun events, but they also must be properly roleplayed.
